science or AI business audiences rather than infrastructure. AI in Healthcare,

## Two rules that bind us

1. **VentureBeat and No Jitter ban AI-generated content outright.** No Jitter
   allows assistive tools like spell-check and nothing further. The sentences
   have to be the author's. Same constraint as the paper's open retype item,
   now an outlet rule rather than only a personal standard.
2. **Exclusivity.** Submit to one outlet, wait for the answer, then move down
   the list. Simultaneous submission gets you blacklisted at TechTarget titles.

## What makes a good trade article, per the doc

By professionals for professionals, in an industry-specific outlet. Use
field-standard terms and acronyms freely, include real specs and benchmarks,
assume the reader wants the technical detail, compare directly to known
standards or competitors, stay practical. Our angle already does all five.

## Evidence to keep for each placement

The doc's EB-1A tab lists what a trade article can support: authorship of
scholarly articles (USCIS accepts industry-focused platforms, not only academic
journals), original contributions of major significance, published material
about the beneficiary, and leading or critical role through recurring columns.
Keep publication details, proof of editorial selection, and readership metrics
for every piece that runs.
